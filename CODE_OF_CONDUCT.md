# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the MoTorch community respectful, inclusive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Accept feedback professionally.
- Give credit for contributions and sources.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, sexualized conduct, doxxing, or publication of another person's private information is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Conduct concerns should be reported through an appropriate private GitHub contact mechanism rather than a public issue when privacy is required.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly represents the project.
